# Release section brief

Readers enter at the guided tour’s authorization stop or at a map overview. The seven lessons follow the approved inventory order. A release coordinator reconciles a version with its tag and publication. The change authority identifies the scope of the production decision. The operator verifies the exact deployed revision before the Service Owner widens exposure. Recovery and data compatibility are separate decisions. The release writer uses the resulting evidence to explain who can use automatic routing.

Use a mapping table, approval record, annotated job, rollout choice table, recovery record, compatibility matrix and reader-facing draft notes respectively. The new data migration diagram belongs to its deep dive. Keep the separate overview-figure inventory for the later Figures gate.
