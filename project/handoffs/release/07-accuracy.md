# Independent accuracy review of the Release section

I read the seven generated Release lessons, the Release entries in the depth plan and inventory, the migration SVG, the canonical dataset and the two generated downloads. I opened the primary references listed on each lesson. I compared fixture identifiers, times and claims of scope with the published records, and challenged the teaching examples with missing data, wrong recipients, conflicting revision states and an existing request changed during migration.

## Findings and corrections requested

1. The first readiness loop could print success when a revision reported healthy and provisioned but also had a failed or degraded running state. It described four minutes as a polling limit without including CLI latency. The revised loop explicitly requires Running, checks that the replica count is numeric and positive, and documents 24 reads with ten-second intervals under the overall twenty-minute job timeout.
2. The verification job could approve an eligible selection, wait for the environment gate, and deploy after authorization had expired or been revoked. Recheck the current authorization and selection after the wait, before modifying the cloud target.
3. The visible migration decision combined new-only consumers with a final comparison, without locating the equality test before new-only writes. A legitimate new-only update can leave the old column stale. Put the equality check at the end of dual writes and make the final gate depend on observed business state and remaining consumers.

## Recheck and final verdict

**Accept.** I checked the rebuilt workflow page and reran the readiness/preflight cases. The protected job now explicitly re-reads the authoritative change immediately before modifying Azure, matches the environment, digest and configuration, and holds on expiration, revocation, replacement or failure to read the decision. It labels the change-system-specific call as omitted from the teaching excerpts. On the rebuilt migration page I compared the matrix, equivalent text, visible SVG and SVG description. The served SVG matches the reviewed source asset byte for byte. All three findings are closed.
