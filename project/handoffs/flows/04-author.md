# Six records to follow

Start the Jira page from any numbered stage and identify the status mover, decision owner and evidence. The CI page follows an illustrative failed check on PR-42. The QA page follows the earlier recorded CC-131 failure and retest, then distinguishes the independently qualified integration build. The UAT page follows a mapping correction to a new business decision. The production page follows INC-7 through containment, repair and reconciliation. The traceability page supports reading both from commit to production and backwards from a controlled production check.

Each page has an illustrative setting, sample output, named owner, failure and transfer note. The Jira table uses the seven map headings. The five other pages pair diagrams with text equivalents. The code repair alternatives in UAT and production are hypothetical. The public test-management example is Kiwi TCMS 17.2, documented and source-checked, with no claim of a live Kiwi/Jira exercise.
