# Author handoff

Six lessons follow CC-100 to CC-123, the team's readiness decision, the proposed routing design and the evidence for its criteria. Each opens with the business question, then provides a decision record, example fields, a checklist or a trace table. The lessons distinguish the recommended design from recorded qualification. No custom executable script is used in a Define lesson. The criteria trace includes the Kiwi version, documentation and published-source basis, and the absence of a live Kiwi–Jira demonstration.
