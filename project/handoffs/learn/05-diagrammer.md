On success, the team verifies affected work, holds wider exposure until QA passes the deferred export test and the Service Owner reviews the pilot and ensures the shipped repair is present on main. The author received the figure block with a caption, alternative text and a prose route through both forks.

I inspected both views of each figure, following each branch, junction, boxed label and free caption.
