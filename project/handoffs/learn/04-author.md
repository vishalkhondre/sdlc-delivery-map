# Six Learn lessons

The authors covered observing and alerting, responding to incidents and an emergency code fix, and interpreting product evidence and opening follow-up work. The logs, notification and configuration samples are explicitly illustrative. The event sample lets a reader follow a committed first assignment by request, event sequence and serving configuration and compare it with an independently approved routing rule. The emergency example has no new historical incident or digest invented for the supplied record.

The outcome lesson includes a runnable Python example and a documented Power Query alternative. Running the example against the original CSV produced its displayed output. Among all 100 purchases the correct-assignment halfway milestone was 11 minutes; the one-day decision halfway milestone was unreached. The final lesson separates the Service Owner’s investigation from the Product Owner’s proposed supporting-details experiment and its own acceptance check, measurement and review.

The reviews and any subsequent corrections are recorded in the independent handoffs.
