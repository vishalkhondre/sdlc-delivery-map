An incident lead contains the failure, and the Service Owner accounts for affected requests. The reader then rehearses a separate urgent code repair. A business analyst measures the later cohort and the Product Owner records a decision on the epic.

The expected artifacts are a correlated event, a rule and notification, an incident timeline and review, a hypothetical hotfix diagram, a repeatable calculation from the downloadable CSV, and a proposed CC-140 record.
