# Changelog

## 1.1.0

Follow the approval-routing feature through 40 new deep dives and six end-to-end flows. The 23 overview lessons with deeper material link to worked examples of CI failures, defect reports, UAT decisions, release authorization, production recovery and product feedback. The tour and searchable index offer routes into each deeper lesson.

Four overview figures and two decision tables show how to trace evidence, repeat qualification, interpret the one-day cohort, read map arrows and distinguish environment decisions. The printable guide includes all 99 lessons, figures with text descriptions, and the worked JSON and CSV as attachments.

## 1.0.2

Distinguish the pilot routing window from the later product cohort, with ownership for outstanding assignments. Search now includes lesson instructions and failure paths. The home title covers the full journey, each footer identifies its edition and commit, and the topic filter is labelled Section. Lessons return to their map cards, product feedback links to retirement, and all nine acceptance-testing references point to the ISTQB syllabus.

## 1.0.1

The site carries the diagram’s phase colours and activity names into the guided tour and lessons. The navy header, shorter headings, narrower reading column and typeset technical identifiers make it easier to follow a card into its explanation on desktop and mobile.

## 1.0.0

The first delivery-map edition follows a fictional approval-routing feature from the product decision through production and outcome review. It includes an interactive swimlane, a text route through the lessons, decision and recovery paths, examples of handoff records and public references. Each section can be updated through the same validation and release pipeline.
